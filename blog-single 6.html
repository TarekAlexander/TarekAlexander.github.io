Dexter: Red Cards!

  • By: Individual work
  • Date: Nov 1, 2024

Problem
Dexter: Red Cards explored how smell can be used to create doubt, deduction, and decision-making. I wanted players to experience the tension of trusting or questioning a sensory clue when other information might be misleading.

My Role & Process
I designed the core mechanics, wrote the rules, and created the scent clue cards and Red Cards. During testing, I watched how players interpreted unclear smells, handled false leads, negotiated with themselves, and decided when to trust instinct over logic.

"Every scent was a clue, but the interesting part was watching players decide whether the clue deserved trust."

Key Decisions & Learning
Early tests showed that some scents were too obvious while others were too confusing. Players also needed clearer instructions for using Red Cards strategically. I simplified scent categories, refined card text, and added a Final Showdown to keep tension high. This project helped me think about ambiguity as a design material: useful when it creates curiosity, risky when it blocks action.
