I'm

I'm an Interaction Designer focused on UX research, scent interaction, and designing experiences that reveal how people think, respond, and collaborate.

I connect research, prototyping, and visual design to create thoughtful experiences that are clear, useful, and human-centered.

My work combines research through design, prototyping, observation, playtesting, and qualitative analysis. I am especially interested in multisensory interaction: how smell, ambiguity, memory, and conversation can shape participation and meaning making in social contexts.

Case Studies

Smell as a Learning Material

Bachelor thesis case study: using Research through Design to explore how smell shapes participation, turn taking, hesitation, and collaborative meaning making in second language group activities.

Game Design & Playtesting

An iterative playtesting project where I refined rules, scent clues, timing, and player feedback to understand how smell can create uncertainty, social play, and strategy.

Smell Dexter

A scent-detective game concept where I used conflicting clues to explore trust, interpretation, and decision making when sensory information feels uncertain.

(AR) Wound Monitoring

A healthcare concept exploring how AR and sensor-based tracking could support remote wound monitoring, clearer patient communication, and continuity of care.

Climatise Scent

A multisensory prototype exploring how scent and trigeminal stimulation could influence perceived temperature, comfort, and environmental interaction.

Partitect

A planning app concept developed through user feedback, with focus on simplifying event preparation, shopping lists, accessibility, and everyday decision making.

SafePause Vest

A safety concept shaped from warehouse work experience: I explored how wearable prompts could support better break timing, attention, and safer routines in high-pressure logistics environments.
